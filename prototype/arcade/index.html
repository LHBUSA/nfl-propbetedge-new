This is not an exact replay. The feed carries no X/Y player tracking. The snap spot, the destination, the down and distance, the possession change and the result are taken from the published play and are exact. Routes, running lanes, blocking, defender movement, formation and all 22 player positions are reconstructed for illustration and are not historical tracking data. Player identities are read out of the published play text because the feed has no structured participant list — they are marked TEXT_DERIVED below. The offence always attacks right in this view; that is a presentation choice, not the real field orientation.
