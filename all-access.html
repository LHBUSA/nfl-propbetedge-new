GOOD TO KNOW

How All Access works

Is PropBetEdge Predictions a sport?
No. It is a separate intelligence product — independent, source-backed forecasts with model probability, market comparison and a scored record. All Access includes it beside the 10 sports.
I only follow football.
NFL Pro remains available on its own from your NFL account. All Access is the option for readers who want the rest of the network too.
Already an NFL Pro member?
Your NFL desk stays exactly as it is. Upgrading adds the other nine sport desks and PropBetEdge Predictions to the same account.
How is access verified?
Passwordless: a single-use sign-in link to the email on your membership. Access is decided on the server from your active membership, never in the browser.
